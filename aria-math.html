Aria Math

Aria Math was an assignment to create a pavillion which could be experienced along side nature. I responded to this assignemnt by designing a structure that harmonizes with its natural surroundings by use of rain drums scattered around the structure. I decided to play with the idea that sound can create memories that people feel without knowing.

Soundtrack Inspiration

The title of this project was taken from a track off of the soundtrack for the game Minecraft (by C418). Its handpan-drum motif gives this song a natural feeling. It inspired my use of rain drums for a memorable sound experience.
